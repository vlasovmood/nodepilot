# Code of Conduct

We want taking part in NodePilot to be a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Discuss code and decisions, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole, not only for yourself.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Harassment, in public or in private messages.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, contacts, server details) without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), in the project's chats and at
its events, and whenever someone officially represents the project.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers clarify the standards of acceptable behavior and respond fairly to violations. They may remove or edit
comments, commits, code and issues, and may restrict participation temporarily or permanently.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainers privately through the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/vlasovmood).
All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

## Enforcement

1. **Correction:** a private, written note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** with consequences for repeated behavior, including limited interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** from participating in the project.
4. **Permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment or aggression.
